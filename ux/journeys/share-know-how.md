# Journey: Share know-how or a skill

[← UX reviews](../README.md) · Personas: [Translator](../personas/translator.md), [Skill builder](../personas/skill-builder.md)

**Goal:** a person shares what they know (a guide, a style file, a prompt, a skill, a paper) so that others can use it, and gets credit. This is the **Share** step of Share → Measure → Release.

**Done when:** the entry is listed in the [prompt library](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/buddhist-prompt-library) (or a Papers page in the knowledge base) and the author sees their name on it and in the contributors row (or the entry without a name, if they asked not to be shown).

## Steps today (2026-11-18)

Design: [Share a skill or any file you give to AI](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/buddhistai.github.io/blob/main/docs/designs/2026-11-18-share-any-ai-file.md).

| # | Where | What the person does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Homepage](https://buddhistai.github.io/): hero line, Contribute box, or "Share yours" on the Art, practice and teaching cards | Reads "Share your files: a skill, project instructions, a Claude file, a style guide or a prompt", and that it needs a free GitHub account and the form is public. |
| 2 | [Share your files](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/buddhist-prompt-library/issues/new?template=share.yml) form (prompt library) | Picks what it is (personal AI file, project instructions, style guide, glossary, prompt, skill, workflow, other) and pastes a link or uploads files. Name and picture are optional. |
| 3 | Maintainer | Adds a prompt library entry, or a [Papers](../../papers/README.md) page for a paper or guide to read, and credits the author ([how-to](../../handbook/how-to.md#turn-a-share-issue-into-a-prompt-library-entry)). A skill for a task with a benchmark is also added as a variant in its workshop repo. |
| 4 | GitHub message, then the [prompt library](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/buddhist-prompt-library) list | The author gets a message with the link, finds their entry and their name in the contributors row. |

## Pages this journey touches

Homepage hero, cards and Contribute section, the Share your files form, the prompt library README, knowledge-base Papers, the contributors row.

## Known problems

- Sharing without a GitHub account is not possible yet ([Status](../../handbook/status.md)).
