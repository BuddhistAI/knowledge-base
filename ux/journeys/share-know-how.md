# Journey: Share know-how or a skill

[← UX reviews](../README.md) · Personas: [Translator](../personas/translator.md), [Skill builder](../personas/skill-builder.md)

**Goal:** a person shares what they know (a guide, a style file, a prompt, a skill, a paper) so that others can use it, and gets credit. This is the **Share** step of Share → Measure → Release.

**Done when:** the entry is listed (a community entry in buddhist-skills or a paper in the knowledge base) and the author sees their name on it.

## Steps today (2026-11-18)

| # | Where | What the person does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Homepage](https://buddhistai.github.io/), Share section | Pastes a link, or reads "No GitHub repo? Upload your files." |
| 2 | [Share form](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/buddhist-skills/issues/new?template=share.yml) | Fills in the GitHub issue form with a link or uploaded files. Name and picture are optional. Needs a free GitHub account. |
| 3 | Maintainer | Turns the issue into a `community/` entry or a [Papers](../../papers/README.md) page and credits the author ([how-to](../../handbook/how-to.md)). |
| 4 | [Community entries](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/buddhist-skills/tree/main/community) | The author finds their entry and their name in the contributors row. |

## Pages this journey touches

Homepage Share section, the Share form, buddhist-skills `community/` and `SKILLS.md`, knowledge-base Papers, the contributors row.

## Known problems

- Sharing without a GitHub account is not possible yet ([Status](../../handbook/status.md)).
