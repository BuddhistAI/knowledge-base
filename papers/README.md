# Papers

Papers and method notes that people in the community wrote about AI for Buddhist tasks. Each paper has a short page in our own words, the full text in Markdown (so an AI can read it), and the author's PDF.

These are the authors' own work, under their own license. We do not edit them. They are not measured results: a paper may suggest a benchmark or a skill, and that work then happens in buddhist-benchmarks or buddhist-skills.

To share a paper, open a [Share a workflow](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/knowledge-base/issues/new?template=share-workflow.yml) issue or post in [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/BuddhistAI/discussions).
