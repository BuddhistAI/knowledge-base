# <Change in a few words>

## Problem

What is wrong or missing today, for whom (which persona), and how we know (a link to feedback, an issue or a walkthrough).

## Change

What the person will see and do after the change, step by step, in the persona's words. Include the exact words on buttons and headings.

## Journeys and pages

For each journey in `journeys`: the steps before and after. If the steps change, update `ux/journeys/<id>.md` in the same pull request.

## Files

The files that change, and why.

## Not in scope

What this change does not do.

## Goldfish review

Filled in after the review: date, reviewers that ran, open Blocker and Major findings, and what was accepted as a known risk.
