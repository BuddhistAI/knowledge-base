# Buddhist knowledge Q&A

Answer questions on Buddhist doctrine, history and practice correctly for a given tradition.

## Definition

Answering a question about Buddhism the way a qualified teacher of the relevant tradition would, and attributing views to the right tradition when traditions differ. This measures models' knowledge rather than a text-processing skill.

## Input and output

- **Input:** A question, optionally with the tradition it is asked within.
- **Output:** An answer, judged against reference answers reviewed by people of that tradition.

## Examples

None yet. Add a short real example with its correct answer, and say where it comes from.

## Edge cases

- Questions where traditions disagree.
- Common misattributions and popular misquotations.
- Questions asked in languages other than English.

## Languages and traditions

- Question language is an axis of the benchmark: the same question asked in different languages.

## How it is measured

Benchmark: [BuddhistBench](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/buddhist-bench), a question-and-answer benchmark with tracks by tradition, exam papers and single texts. It is one benchmark among others.

## Help wanted

Questions and reviewed answers from your tradition, and exam papers you have permission to share. A first benchmark can be small: twenty to fifty items. See [how to submit a benchmark](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/buddhist-benchmarks/blob/main/SUBMIT.md).
